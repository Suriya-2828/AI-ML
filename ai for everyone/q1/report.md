# Customer Sales Analysis Report

## 1. Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer sales transactions using Python and Pandas. The dataset is cleaned by handling missing values and duplicate records. The cleaned data is then analyzed to compute revenue by product category, identify the top 10 customers based on sales, and visualize monthly sales trends.

---

## 2. Dataset

**Dataset Used:** Sample Superstore Dataset (Kaggle)

The dataset contains sales transaction records with information such as:

* Order ID
* Order Date
* Customer Name
* Product Category
* Product Name
* Sales
* Quantity
* Discount
* Profit
* Region
* State

---

## 3. Libraries Used

* pandas
* matplotlib

---

## 4. Data Cleaning

The following preprocessing steps were performed:

* Loaded the CSV dataset using Pandas.
* Removed duplicate records.
* Checked for missing values.
* Filled missing values (if any) in Customer Name, Category, Sales, and Quantity columns.
* Converted the "Order Date" column into DateTime format.
* Saved the cleaned dataset as `cleaned_sales_data.csv`.

---

## 5. Summary Statistics

Summary statistics were generated using the `describe()` function to obtain:

* Count
* Mean
* Standard Deviation
* Minimum
* Maximum
* Quartiles (25%, 50%, 75%)

These statistics provide an overview of numerical columns such as Sales, Quantity, Discount, Profit, and Postal Code.

---

## 6. Revenue by Product Category

The total sales revenue was calculated by grouping transactions according to product category.

The three product categories are:

* Technology
* Furniture
* Office Supplies

The analysis identifies which category contributes the highest revenue.

---

## 7. Top 10 Customers

Customer sales were grouped by Customer Name and sorted in descending order.

The top ten customers based on total sales revenue were identified to determine the most valuable customers.

---

## 8. Data Visualization

Two visualizations were generated:

### Plot 1 – Monthly Sales

A line graph showing monthly sales revenue across the entire dataset.

Purpose:

* Observe monthly sales trends.
* Identify seasonal fluctuations.

### Plot 2 – Revenue by Product Category

A bar chart comparing total revenue generated by each product category.

Purpose:

* Compare category-wise sales performance.

---

## 9. Output Files

The program generates the following files:

* cleaned_sales_data.csv
* monthly_sales.png
* category_revenue.png
* top10_customers.png (optional)

---

## 10. Conclusion

The project successfully demonstrates the use of Python for data analysis. Using Pandas, the dataset was cleaned and analyzed to calculate category-wise revenue and identify the highest-value customers. Matplotlib was used to visualize monthly sales trends and category revenue, providing meaningful insights into business performance.
